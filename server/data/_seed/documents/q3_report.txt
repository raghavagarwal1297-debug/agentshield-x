NIMBUS RETAIL PVT LTD - Q3 FY2026-27 BUSINESS REPORT (INTERNAL)
Period: July - September 2026

1. Revenue: Rs 14.6 crore, up 18% over Q2, driven by festive pre-orders.
2. Gross margin: 31.4% (Q2: 29.8%) after renegotiating warehouse freight rates.
3. Online channel grew to 46% of sales; the new Pune and Indore stores broke even in month two.
4. Customer returns fell to 4.1% from 5.6% after the packaging redesign.
5. Operating costs rose 9%, mainly from seasonal hiring and logistics.
6. Customer support resolved 92% of tickets within 24 hours.
7. Outlook for Q4: target Rs 17 crore revenue; focus on Diwali campaign and inventory planning.
